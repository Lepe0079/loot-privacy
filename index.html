Privacy Policy — Loot

Last updated: July 20, 2026

Loot ("the app") is an Android app for tracking a Magic: The Gathering card collection and checking card prices by voice, photo, or text. This page explains what data the app handles and how.

No accounts, no sign-up

Loot has no user accounts, no login, and no server-side profile. There is nothing to register and nothing tied to your identity.

Your collection data stays on your device

Your card collection — names, quantities, conditions, and anything imported from a ManaBox CSV export — is stored locally on your device only, in a private on-device database. It is never uploaded to us or to any third party. Uninstalling the app deletes this data permanently; there is no account to delete because none exists.

Voice input

When you use the microphone to speak a card name, the audio is processed by Android's built-in speech recognition service (typically provided by Google) to produce a text transcript. Loot only receives the resulting text, not a copy of the audio recording.

Photo scan

The photo-scan feature reads a card's set symbol and collector number from a photo you take. This is processed entirely on-device using Google's ML Kit text recognition — photos are not uploaded or stored by Loot.

Card lookups

To fetch card details and prices, Loot sends the card name (or set code and collector number) you're looking up to one of:

  • Scryfall, a free public Magic: The Gathering card database, directly from your device; or
  • Loot's own backend service, which mirrors a subset of Scryfall's data and is gated by Google's Play Integrity API rather than a user account — see below.

Only the card name/query text is sent. No personal information, device identifiers, or collection data is included in these requests.

Play Integrity

Requests to Loot's own backend are verified using Google Play Integrity, which confirms a request comes from a genuine, unmodified install of Loot on a certified device. This is a device/app attestation check, not an account system — it does not identify you personally and nothing is stored tied to a persistent user or device ID.

Optional AI-assisted matching

Loot has an optional, off-by-default setting that uses Anthropic's Claude API to help match misheard card names. If you turn this on, you supply your own Anthropic API key, which is stored securely on your device (Android's encrypted keystore, via expo-secure-store) and never sent to us. When this setting is enabled, the text you're trying to match is sent directly from your device to Anthropic's API — see Anthropic's privacy policy. This feature is entirely optional and does nothing unless you enable it and provide a key.

Advertising

Loot shows ads via Google AdMob. AdMob may collect device identifiers (such as the advertising ID) and other data to serve and measure ads, per Google's own policies. See How Google uses information from sites or apps that use Google services and Google's Privacy Policy.

What we don't do

  • We don't sell your data.
  • We don't build advertising profiles beyond what Google AdMob does on its own (see above).
  • We don't collect your collection data, your voice recordings, or your photos on any server we control.

Children's privacy

Loot is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from them.
